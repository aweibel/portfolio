# Question bank

Pick the 3 to 5 questions that would improve this draft most, and rewrite them in the user's own terms (their product, teammates and features). Ask them as numbered, open questions in plain prose, so they can be answered by voice in any order. Don't ask about what they've already told you.

## Impact and metrics (ask first when impact is thin)

People rarely remember numbers while talking, so make the questions easy to answer:

- "What changed after launch that you could point to? For example, usage, completion, time, errors, support tickets, revenue, retention or satisfaction."
- For each metric, ask for all four parts together, so one answer covers them:
  - the **baseline** (before)
  - the **result** (after)
  - the **timeframe** (e.g. the first 30 or 90 days)
  - **how it was measured** (analytics, A/B test, survey, support data, sales data)
- "Where could you look it up?" Suggest specific places:
  - product analytics (Amplitude, Mixpanel, GA, Pendo)
  - the launch announcement or release notes
  - QBR or exec decks
  - the support ticket system
  - the CRM or sales notes
  - an old Slack thread
  - asking the PM or data partner
- If exact numbers are gone, offer **proxies**:
  - the number of teams, customers or products that adopted it
  - time saved per task × frequency
  - tickets or escalations avoided
  - deals it unblocked
  - designers or engineers using the system
  - NPS or CSAT verbatims
- Also offer **directional framing**, but only with the user's confirmation ("roughly halved", "from weeks to days").
- "Did it make money, save money, or reduce risk for the business? Who noticed?"
- "Is there a quote from a customer, an exec or a teammate about the result?"
- If it didn't ship or was cancelled: "What did the work change anyway? A decision, a strategy, a later product, the team's process?"

## Tensions (the soft skills and politics)

- "Was there a moment when someone strongly disagreed with the direction? Who was it (role is fine), and what did they want?"
- "Why was their view reasonable from where they sat?"
- "How did you work it out? With data, a prototype, a test, a compromise, an escalation, or by giving ground?"
- "What did you give up to get the important part through?"
- "How was the relationship afterwards?"
- "Were there tensions between teams, such as design vs engineering capacity, product vs sales, or brand vs usability?"

## Setbacks

- "What did you try that didn't work, and how did you find out?"
- "Was there a concept you loved that got cut? Why?"
- "Did a test or launch surprise you?"
- "What would have failed if you'd shipped your first idea?"
- "What got cut from scope, and how did you decide?"

## Research and empathy

- "Who did you learn from: how many people, in what roles, and how (interviews, shadowing, analytics, support tickets)?"
- "Describe one real person you designed for. What was their day like, and where did it hurt?"
- "Is there a sentence a user said that stuck with you?"
- "What did the research show that you or the team didn't expect, and what did it change in the design?"
- "Were there different types of users with conflicting needs? How did you serve both?"

## Context and role

- "What was at stake for the business if this didn't get fixed?"
- "Why did this get prioritised then?"
- "What constraints did you work under: deadline, legacy tech, legal, headcount?"
- "What exactly did you own, and what did others own? Who were your key partners?"
- "How big was the team?"

## Process and solution

- "What were the two or three directions you considered, and why did this one win?"
- "How did you validate it before shipping?"
- "What detail are you proudest of that most people wouldn't notice?"
- "Which screens or artifacts still exist that we could show? (Old Figma files, recordings, slides, photos of whiteboards.)"

## Retro

- "Knowing what you know now, what would you do differently?"
- "What did this project teach you that you've used since?"
- "What happened to it after you left or finished?"

## Images and shot list

When suggesting images, say exactly what to capture and how to annotate it. For example: "Before/after of the setup screen, side by side, with the 3 removed steps circled." If there's an NDA, suggest recreating the screens with generic branding, blurring, or showing process artifacts instead.
