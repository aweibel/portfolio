so this one's under NDA so be careful. it was for Halvorsen Mutual, the big insurance company, through the agency I was at, Fieldwork Studio, in 2021. I was senior product designer. we redesigned the claims intake for their agents, the internal tool agents use when someone calls in a car accident. it was a green screen era thing basically, agents had like six tabs open and were retyping the same policy number. I shadowed agents in their call center in Omaha for two days, and we did eight interviews. the insight was agents were scared of making mistakes on recorded calls, so they went slow and double checked everything. we designed a single intake screen that pulls the policy as soon as you type the number and highlights what's missing. the client's IT team fought us on the live lookup because of mainframe load, we compromised on a cached nightly policy snapshot with a refresh button. a first version with a chat-style guided flow flopped in testing, agents found it slow. it launched to one region first. average handle time went down, the client told us it was meaningful but I never got the number. I learned to get metrics access written into the contract next time.
